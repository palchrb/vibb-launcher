Settings

Each switch saves itself immediately.

Emergency numbers (112, 110, 113) always work, whether or not they are in the phone book. Add them below if you want buttons for them. The offline-override PIN and "pause all restrictions" do not open calls.

Calls from other numbers are rejected, but the carrier may still send them to voicemail: dial ##004# on the phone once to turn off forwarding to voicemail, and don't allow the voicemail number. Directory and connect services (1881, 1880, ...) can put a call through to any number, so don't add them. A caller can fake a contact's number; the phone rejects calls the network marks as failed verification.
