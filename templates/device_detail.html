Phone hardening

Android restrictions the launcher sets while this phone is managed. Each switch saves itself immediately. The offline-override PIN and "pause all restrictions" don't lift these - only turning a switch off here does.

With USB debugging blocked, a computer can't repair the phone: if the launcher ever breaks so badly that it can't reach this server, the only way back is a reset from the recovery menu and setting the phone up again. Turn it off here before you work on the phone with adb.

Off by default. Safe mode starts the phone without the launcher, so the kid can get around it - but it's also the way past a launcher that crashes at start. Only turn this on once this launcher version has been tested on the phone, and preferably with USB debugging still allowed.

Quick Controls (swipe-left screen)

These appear on a screen the kid can reach by swiping left from the home screen. This is the intended way for a kid to reach these controls instead of Android's own Quick Settings shade, which can't be fully locked down - a third-party app can publish its own tile there (Tailscale does) that bypasses restrictions below.

Ad/content filtering (VPN)

The launcher runs its own on-device VPN to filter ads/content locally (see the DNS/Filters tab for blocklists and what's been blocked). Turning this off disables that filtering on this device entirely - the device still syncs normally, it just stops blocking anything. There's no on-device way for a kid to turn this off themselves.

Offline override PIN

A failsafe for when the phone can't reach the server at all. Entering this PIN directly on the phone lifts the app restrictions and the schedule for up to two hours (not calls or phone hardening); they come back the moment the phone can sync again. The same PIN opens the launcher's own Settings on the phone - without a PIN, those Settings can't be opened at all.
