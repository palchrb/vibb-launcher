A Vibb box's library.json, at most 1 MB. Nothing is saved before you confirm.

Its podcasts and NRK series become entries here, each Vibb section a category. Copy the file from the box first, for example with scp <pi>:/etc/vibb/library.json . (the box's GET /library answer works too).

Nothing is fetched while importing. Storytel and Spotify entries come over too (Spotify ones on no phone yet); Spotify short links and profiles, the box's own folders, positions and downloads don't.
