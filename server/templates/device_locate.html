Wipe…
This erases the device completely and can't be undone. There's no way to confirm afterward that it actually happened, since a wiped device can't check back in.

Lock: with a kid's PIN set (Screen lock card on the device page), locks with the kid's PIN and turns the screen off; calls from allowed contacts and 112 still work. Without one, it only turns the screen off ("locked (Android)" in the result).
