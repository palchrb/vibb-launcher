Add a photo

A photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB) is turned upright, cut to the shape of a phone screen, made smaller and saved again without any hidden information such as where it was taken. It is stored on this server and, once you tick it on a phone's page, on that phone, where the child sees it and can choose it as the home-screen background. A new photo is on no phone until you tick it there.
