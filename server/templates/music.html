Music

The library for the Vibb music app: podcasts, NRK series and your own files, sorted into categories. Pick what each phone gets on that phone's page (Music card). The phones fetch podcasts and NRK straight from the source; this server only keeps the list and your own files.

Add from a link

Paste an NRK radio link (radio.nrk.no/podkast/... or radio.nrk.no/serie/...) or a podcast feed (RSS). The server checks the link once and takes its title as the name.

Storytel

The family's Storytel login, for the phones where it's switched on (each phone's Music card). It is kept encrypted on this server and never shown again - not here, not in a log. Audiobooks from Storytel come in a later version of the music app.
