Locks the phone when the screen turns off.

Calls from allowed contacts and emergency calls work while locked. After 5 wrong PINs the phone waits 30 s, then 1, 2, 5 and 15 minutes between tries. It never erases itself. Your Parent PIN ("Parent code" on the lock) always opens it.

This lock keeps people nearby out. It does not protect the phone's data from a thief with forensic tools, and some bank or wallet apps won't work.
