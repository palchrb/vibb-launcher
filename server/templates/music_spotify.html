Spotify on the phones: your family's own Spotify app and each child's account.

Spotify plays on the phones through your family's own Spotify developer app and your children's Spotify accounts: one account per phone, the child's own. Your own Spotify account is never logged in here - you paste the links.

This server ships no app of its own: set yours up once, then log in each child's account. Until then nothing else in the music library changes.

How to Set It Up
  1. Log in at developer.spotify.com/dashboard with a Premium account (a Development-mode app needs a Premium owner; if that Premium lapses, the app stops working until it resumes) and create an app. Name: anything not starting with "Spot", for example "Vibb hjemme". Website: empty.
  2. Redirect URI: the exact value shown above{% if !configured %} once saved (for this address: {{ suggested }}){% endif %}. APIs used: Web API and Web Playback SDK.
  3. User Management: add each child's Spotify account - one per phone, the child's own, never yours - by name and e-mail. At most {{ dev_mode_users }} accounts can use a Development-mode app; Spotify only grants more to organisations with 250k monthly users.
  4. Copy the Client ID (not the Client secret: this server doesn't need it) into the field above and save.
  5. The limits are your app's own: logins have to be renewed every 6 months (this page reminds you), a playlist's tracks list only for its owner or a collaborator, Spotify's own editorial playlists aren't available to developer apps, and Spotify's Developer Policy forbids apps "targeted to children" - using this for your child is at your own risk; Spotify may switch the app off.
  6. Playlists: a phone shows a playlist's tracks only when a child's account here owns it or collaborates on it - make it in the Spotify app while logged in as the child, or invite the child to collaborate on yours (in Spotify, the playlist's ⋯ › Invite collaborators; then open the invite link while logged in as the child - the private window you logged the child in from - and join). Then "Check Now" on its page lists it. Any other playlist plays in Spotify's order without a track list, and only if the child's account can open it (public, or shared with the child). Albums, tracks, artists and podcasts need nothing.

One account per phone, the child's own - never yours.
