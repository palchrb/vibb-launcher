# 2026-10-23 - design 59d §8.4: G's mid-round capture (does a window's chrome pass cancel Android's pad?)

Branch `d59d-round1` after steps C, N1, H, B (with its follow-up) and K (`acc306aa`; K's revision `d033537b` changes
only the wallpaper and came after the run). Question (§8.4, RC F2 in `2026-10-22-d59-rc-run.md`): with Android's PIN
pad open from "Lås opp", the PWA ends a parent window - which lock-task write precedes the pad's `cancelled`?

**Verdict: the pad was not cancelled.** Four runs, kiosk off once and kiosk on three times (with the kiosk on, the
end of the pause changes the lock-task packages from the kiosk-off LOCKED list to the kiosk list, and writes the
features), with the end posted 2.5 s (runs 1 and 2), 0.3 s (run 3) and 0 s (run 4) after the tap. Each time the window's whole chrome pass and the relock ran under the open
pad, and the bouncer kept the focus (`NotificationShade`) for the 10-13 s sampled afterwards. No chrome-pass DPM write
cancels the pad on this build, so **G is not built** (§8.4).

The likely cause of RC F2 is the relock's own Home start, not the chrome: the relock brings Home to the front (24b
step 6), and on the RC build that start ran at once, over the keyguard. Since 59d step A (59b's code-QA fix round) it
waits for Android's unlock - in every run here `KeyguardCover: Home (the window locked again) waits for Android's
unlock` comes right before `WindowRelock: Relocked`, and the pad stays. Not proven: the RC build isn't re-run here.
