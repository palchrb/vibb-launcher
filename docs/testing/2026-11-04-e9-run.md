## Findings

**M1 - The lock tile's transport doesn't reach Fossify Music Player** (Medium; `launcher/.../music/MusicSessions.kt:335-353`
`playPause`/`next`/`previous`). The platform dispatched the launcher's `TransportControls.pause()` (and `skipToNext`) to
Fossify's Media3 session, which stayed PLAYING; a media key (`cmd media_session dispatch pause`) paused it at once, and
the same `pause()` from `pauseBeforeSuspend` did pause it under a suspension. Vibb music and Fennec obey the tile. Cause
not pinned (Fossify 1.8.5's Media3 session accepts every controller in `onConnectAsync`); a real kid's player may behave
so. *Fix idea:* send the tile's actions as media buttons on the session (`controller.dispatchMediaButtonEvent` with
`KEYCODE_MEDIA_PAUSE`/`PLAY`/`NEXT`/`PREVIOUS`, down and up), or fall back to that when the state hasn't changed within
~1 s; check Spotify/YouTube/NRK on the Jelly Star.

**M2 - A podcast player without `appCategory` never shows** (Medium, a product call; `LockSessionPick.kt:70`
`lockMediaApp`). AntennaPod 3.12.3 declares no category, so its playing session never reaches the tile. This is QA M1 of
`lock-media-apps-2` confirmed on a real app; the approved fix route is a parent/server override, not a looser rule.

**L1 - The refusal toast is cut** (Low; `SettingsFragmentLauncher.kt:358` and `:693`, nb `values-nb/strings.xml:203`).
Android 12+ toasts show two lines, so "Telefonen bruker bare en serveradresse som begynner med https:// (vanlig http..."
ends there; "Bruk serverens https-adresse." is never seen. The row summary (shot 42) has the full text. *Fix:* a short
toast ("Bare https://-adresser") or an inline error in the dialog.

**L2 - The own-line alarm glyph keeps its old size after a font change** (Low; glance QA L2, `LockClockFace.kt:200` -
the own line's guard compares text only; the date-line copy at :192 has `lastAlarmPx`). Visible at 1.3 -> 2.0.

**L3 - "Nødsamtale" breaks mid-word at font 2.0** (Low, pre-existing; `layout/view_lock_glance.xml:570-575`, 52 dp margins
on both sides): a fresh lock activity at 2.0 draws "Nødsamtal / e" (shot 34). The emergency call's label should never
split; `maxLines=1` with auto-size, or smaller margins at large fonts.

**L4 - The card's spoken text carries the soft hyphen** (Low; `WeatherLockPanel.kt:559` takes `weather_part_afternoon`
= `Etter­middag`, `values-nb/strings.xml:514`): the description reads "Etter\xadmiddag: Klarvær. ...". TTS likely
skips U+00AD, but strip it for the spoken text.

**L5 - "Oppdatert" reads like a label missing its time** (Low, nb wording; `server/src/i18n/nb.json:2909`
`settings.updates.up_to_date`): alone on its row it reads "Updated (when?)". "Nyeste versjon" / "Du har nyeste versjon".

**L6 - The sound picker's back arrow says "Tilbake til hjemskjermen"** (Low, shared header; `layout/include_kid_header.xml:22`)
but returns to Innstillinger; the same on the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pickers. A "Tilbake" description for sub-pages.

**L7 - Read-only sound rows keep their chevron** (Low, cosmetic; `activity_kid_settings.xml:234`, `:282`).

**L8 - "Kveldshimmel" wraps mid-word on Device › Hjem-skjerm at 390 px** (Low, pre-existing; the wallpaper grid label,
`nb.json:3702`): "Kveldshimm / el".
