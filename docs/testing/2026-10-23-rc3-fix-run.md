## Setup

- **Builds.** The debug launcher of this branch (`0.23.6-debug`), and the server of this branch on port 3140 with the
  debug admin component. No music app.
- **Emulator.** `handy36` (Android 16, Pixel SystemUI), `-wipe-data -no-window -no-audio -gpu swiftshader_indirect`,
  port 5556, `wm density 240`, English, UTC. No other emulator ran.
- **Provisioning.** As the rc.3 run: `emulator.md` §4 by adb, then the PWA scripted over HTTP (Parent PIN, Child PIN
  `1357`, calls managed with one contact, Block USB debugging off, kiosk on), Vibb One enrolled from its Settings, the
  generated `Skumring` photo uploaded, ticked as the phone's only wallpaper and chosen as the lock background, and the
  Lock row set to Android's lock (`PIN command: pin set (rev 1, secure now=true)`).
- **A setup mistake, redone.** The first provisioning opened Vibb One's Settings without ever starting its Home, so
  `internal.started_before` was never written. The first unlocked start after a reboot then ran `resetPreferences`
  ("Resetting preferences"): the enrollment was gone and the hardening defaults came back (`no_debugging_features`, so
  `adb install` failed). This is not a phone path - QR provisioning and §4's "Press Home" start Home first. The AVD
  was wiped and provisioned again, with our Home started first (`am start -a MAIN -c HOME -p ...`). Boots b1 and e1
  below come from the first provisioning, r1-r6 from the second.
- **An emulator wedge.** Once, a Gradle build next to the running emulator left adb `offline` for good (7.4 GB of RAM
  were free before the build, 1.9 GB during it). The emulator was killed from its console and restarted without
  `-wipe-data`; that cold start is e1. Every later build ran with the emulator stopped, and each restart was a cold boot
  (r5, r6).
- **Logs.** The main, system and events buffers with epoch times, across reboots. Recordings: `adb emu screenrecord` at
  24 fps, full-size frames, the photo ridge's y per frame (`ridge.py`, x = 10, 40, 100, 460).

## RC3-1: Android's pad after a boot, with no key press

Each boot below had no input at all until the check: `boot.sh` reboots, waits for `sys.boot_completed`, then reads the
screen at +30 s and +45 s (`pinEntry` or "PIN is required after device restarts" in a UI dump). Times are from the
cover's first ask.

| Boot | How | Build | Keyguard ready (OFF -> LOCKSCREEN) | Pad up | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| b1 | `adb reboot` | first schedule (3/6/12 s, then given up) | ~32 s (cancelled transition, not logged as finished) | 33.2 s | **PASS by luck**: the re-asks at 3/6/12 s were all early; two configuration-change relaunches at 22 s and 28 s restarted the schedule, and their re-ask at 31 s brought the pad |
| e1 | emulator cold start | first schedule | 39.5 s | 115 s, after a power press | **FAIL**: given up at 24 s; RC3-1's dead state (`02-...`: SystemUI's large clock over the cover, a tap reached nothing) |
| r1 | `adb reboot` | final | 26.4 s | 26.9 s | **PASS** |
| r2 | `svc power reboot` | final | ~27 s | 28.0 s | **PASS** |
| r3 | `adb reboot`, `gsm call` ringing from `boot_completed` for 20 s | final | 31.2 s | 32.7 s (1.0 s after the call screen closed) | **PASS** |
| r4 | `adb reboot` | final | 29.2 s | 29.9 s | **PASS** |
| r5 | emulator cold start | final (+ RC3-2 variant B) | ~42 s | 45.6 s | **PASS** |
| r6 | emulator cold start | final (+ RC3-2 variant C) | 42.1 s | 43.4 s | **PASS** |

r1's timeline (the others have the same shape):

```
 +0.0 s  ask resumed, automatic 1 of 3
 +3.1 s  ask no answer, again 1          (dropped: SystemUI's keyguard not booted)
 +6.1 s  ask no answer, again 2
+12.1 s  ask no answer, again 3
+19.1 s  ask relaunched                  (the theme overlays' configuration change)
+23.1 s  ask relaunched
+24.8 s  SystemUI: PrimaryBouncerInteractor#show is being called before the primaryBouncerDelegate is set. Let's exit early
+26.4 s  ask no answer, again 1
+26.4 s  SystemUI: Show primary bouncer requested
+26.4 s  SystemUI: FINISHED OFF -> LOCKSCREEN
+26.9 s  SystemUI: FINISHED -> PRIMARY_BOUNCER  (the pad)
+29.4 s, +35.4 s, +47.4 s ...  ask no answer, again 2, 3, 4 ... (Show primary bouncer requested; harmless)
```

**What the first build taught.** SystemUI's keyguard took its first ask 26-42 s after the cover's first ask on this
emulator, not the 1-3 s the rc.3 run saw (that run's boots came later in a warm VM). With asks only at 3, 6 and 12 s
and a give-up at 24 s, e1 failed exactly like rc.3. The final rule keeps asking every 12 s after the 12 s re-ask until
an answer, a stop or the screen going off. Before SystemUI is ready a request is dropped without a trace ("Let's exit
early"); once it is ready the next one shows the pad. SystemUI processes the asks only from the moment its
`KeyguardViewMediator` starts handling `DISMISS` messages, so an early ask can't be queued for later.

**The first ask's delay (optional in the brief): not built.** The keyguard was ready 26-42 s after the first ask. A
first-frame + 2 s delay would have removed none of the dead asks, and it would delay a healthy boot.

**Calls.** In r3 the call screen (our `InCallActivity`, BFU) came over the cover while it asked. The stop closed the
ask, and nothing was asked during the ring. When the call ended, the cover resumed and asked (`automatic 2 of 3`). The
pad came 1.0 s after the call screen closed, with no key press.

### The mid-typing check

The pad comes up, and the kid types two digits. Re-asks then fire while the pad is up. The kid types the other two
digits and Enter (`midtype.sh`, the Child PIN `1357`).

- **m1 (first build, b1):** a re-ask came with the pad up and nothing typed, then "13", then a re-ask 2.7 s after the
  digits, then "57" and Enter 7.5 s after the digits: `notifyDismissSucceeded(3)`, `Android's PIN pad succeeded`,
  `RUNNING_UNLOCKED`.
- **m2 (final build, r4):** "13", then three re-asks 0.2 s, 3.2 s and 9.2 s after the digits (the ask's 3, 6 and 12 s),
  then "57" and Enter 13.5 s after the digits: `notifyDismissSucceeded(4)`, `RUNNING_UNLOCKED`.

Each re-ask logs `DismissCallbackRegistry: Adding callback` and `Show primary bouncer requested` in SystemUI, and the
bouncer stays as it is. Its two dots stay between the screenshots (`03-...`: the pad, the two dots typed, the two dots
after the re-asks). Since "57" alone is a wrong PIN, the unlock itself proves the first digits survived. **PASS: a
re-ask neither wipes the digits nor closes the pad.**

## RC3-2: the pad's success with a photo ground

The recipe was `padrec.sh`: screen off and on (the glance), the content swipe up, the Child PIN typed, then a 24 fps
recording of Enter. The ridge's y is measured at x = 10.

| Build | Frames where the ground moves | Ridge y per frame around the leave |
|---|---|---|
| Before (this branch without RC3-2) | **3** (f55-57) | 524, 524, 600, 651, (Home over the half-slid ground), 523 (Home) |
| Variant B: `ActivityOptions.makeCustomAnimation(ctx, 0, 0)` on Home's start | **3** (f38-40) | 524, 560, 648, (mixed), 523 |
| Variant C: `FLAG_SHOW_WALLPAPER` on the glance at the leave | **3** | 524, 600, 648, (mixed), 523 |
| **Final (D): `FLAG_SHOW_WALLPAPER` on the glance while it asks** | **0** in two recordings | 523-524 on the glance, 523 on Home, the next frame |

`04-...` (before) and `05-...` (after) show the strips.

Why, from the shell's transition log (`WindowManagerShell`, transition `#23` before, `#17` after):
- Before: `t=OPEN`, Home's task `TO_FRONT f=SHOW_WALLPAPER|NO_ANIMATION`, the glance's task `CLOSE f=NONE`. The
  DefaultTransitionHandler loads `animAttr=0x11` for the glance, `wallpaperOpenExitAnimation`. AOSP 16's
  `wallpaper_open_exit.xml` is a translate to `toYDelta="110%"` in 225 ms, drawn on top: the slide.
- B didn't change it. A custom animation (`ANIM_CUSTOM`) applies to a task change only with
  `getOverrideTaskTransition()`, which no public API sets (AOSP 16 `DefaultTransitionHandler.loadAnimation`).
- C didn't either. WM read the glance's change as `f=NONE` because the flag was added in the same main-thread pass as
  the leave, before any relayout had carried it to WM.
- D: `CLOSE f=SHOW_WALLPAPER`. With both sides showing the wallpaper the transition is the wallpaper-intra kind
  (`wallpaper_intra_open_exit` is an alpha fade with no movement). On these frames the glance's ground gives way to
  Home's in one frame.

The flag check: `fl=... SHOW_WALLPAPER ...` on the glance's window while the pad is up. It is gone after Back on the
pad (the glance's page again), and a second swipe and the PIN still unlocked to Home. `HomeFront.bring`'s
`noAnimation` start is unchanged.

## RC3-3: Back and Home between the PIN and the take-over

There were two runs of the rc.3 run's c1 recipe on the final build. Each started on the pad after a reboot: the PIN,
Back 1 s later, Home at +2.5 s, Back again.

- **r2 (`svc power reboot`, a cover-first boot: `HomePin: HOME resolves to ...BootCoverActivity (take-over)`, then
  `Re-seated ... (1 of 3)`).** The keys were timed on the device in one shell command.
  - Both Backs logged `Boot cover: Back before the take-over, held`.
  - The Home key brought the system's HOME to the cover already in front (`onActivityRestartAttempt`). No new cover
    was made, nothing was finished and nothing was disabled.
  - The cover kept the focus from the PIN to the take-over, 24 s later (`Unlocked setup took 10795 ms` on this debug
    build). Then came `Boot Home started 21836 ms after the unlock` and `Boot cover leaves (taken over, Home over it)`.
  - No `am_anr` and no ANR dialog. No `DevicePolicyEngine` removal and no chooser.
- **r1 (`adb reboot`, a HomeActivity-first boot).** The keys came from the host, so they ran late.
  - Back was held.
  - The Home key started HomeActivity itself, the system's HOME. Our main process was busy then (`Unlocked setup took
    4498 ms`, then Home's create).
  - WM had **no focused window for 8.3 s** (`Focus leaving ... BootCoverActivity, reason=Waiting for window because
    NO_WINDOW`, until HomeActivity's window came).
  - The next Back came after that, so there was no ANR. A key pressed inside that gap and left waiting 5 s would give
    the same `Input dispatching timed out` as c1. That residual isn't the cover's doing: it is the main thread's,
    RC3-4, in PLAN's fix backlog.

Across the whole run there were no ANRs of ours in the recipes. The one `am_anr` of ours was in e1: a service timeout
of `BadgeListenerService` (`waited 20212ms`) before the unlock, in the reset-preferences state after the wedge.
