# Launcher rc.3 candidate - emulator run on Android 16, 2026-10-23

Main at `710d9b9e` (design 59d steps F, round 1 (C, N1, H, B, E, K), J, L, §8.9, M and the RC-to-RC update fix), branch
`rc3-run`. The run covered design 59d §8.4 items 1-8, its "Android's pad size" and "Fingerprint" paragraphs, and the
RC-to-RC fix, on `handy36` (Android 16, Pixel SystemUI). The "AOSP image" paragraph was another agent's. Screenshots are
in `screens/rc3-run/` (480 px wide). Recordings, frames and logs stayed in the scratchpad.

## Setup

- **Builds.** The debug launcher of this commit (versionCode 116, `0.23.6-debug`), and the server of this commit on port
  3130 with the debug admin component. The music app's debug APK came from the J/L run (no `music/` change since
  2026-10-22). Both builds ran before the emulator started.
- **Emulator.** `handy36`, `-wipe-data -no-window -no-audio -gpu swiftshader_indirect`, port 5556, `wm density 240`,
  English, UTC. It started after `aosp36` had stopped, and no other emulator ran during the run.
- **Provisioning and the PWA.** Provisioned per `emulator.md` §4 and enrolled through Vibb One's Settings. The PWA was
  scripted over HTTP:
  - Parent PIN and Child PIN `1357`.
  - Calls managed, with three contacts (Mamma, Pappa, Bestemor).
  - Restrictions at their defaults with Block USB debugging off. The `pwa_setup.py` hardening regex is fixed in this
    copy.
  - Messages, Chrome and Vibb music allowed (kiosk on). The music app was let out with "Allow these apps".
  - Messages on the glance with full text.
  - The Lock row switched to Android's lock (`PIN command: pin set (rev 1, secure now=true)`).
- **Photo wallpaper.** The fixtures have no photo, so a generated dusk picture (`Skumring`, a smooth sky gradient,
  hills, a sun and grain; 1200x2134 JPEG) was uploaded, ticked for the phone and chosen as the lock background.
- **Glance content.**
  - SMS rows from `adb emu sms send`.
  - A missed call from `gsm call` followed by `gsm cancel`.
  - Music from `scripts/fake-audio.py --feed 3`: an RSS entry ticked for the phone and played in the music app.
- **Fingerprint.** Settings is hidden by the device owner, and this production image has no root. So the fingerprint
  was enrolled this way:
  1. A parent pause, and `com.android.settings` ticked for the phone for a moment.
  2. `am start -a android.settings.FINGERPRINT_ENROLL`, then the Child PIN.
  3. 12 `adb emu finger touch 1`. The emulator's sensor is "on the back of your phone", a sensor that isn't under the
     display.
  4. Settings unticked again, and `end-windows`.
- **Logs and recordings.**
  - Logs: the main, system and events buffers with epoch times, kept across reboots.
  - Boot samplers: the top activity and the focus every ~0.4 s for 120-150 s after each reboot.
  - Recordings: `adb emu screenrecord` at 24 fps. Screenshots came from the emulator console, which captures
    `FLAG_SECURE` windows.
- **Notes for the next run.**
  - **No root on `handy36`.** `force-stop` of the device owner is refused ("protected package"). Use
    `run-as me.vibb.launcher.debug kill -9 <pid>` instead. Run-as also writes the cover's device-protected record for
    item 8 (l).
  - **SystemUI's swipe recipe.** `input swipe 240 780 240 250 250` doesn't open SystemUI's pad, not even on its own
    face. `input swipe 240 700 240 100 400` does. SystemUI's face times out after ~10 s, so the PIN has to follow in the
    same command line. On SystemUI's own BFU pad, `input text` didn't reach the field. Tapping the keys did.
  - **The rows' scroll takes a content swipe.** At font 1.3 or density 280 (candidate 5), a swipe that starts on the rows
    scrolls them (by design). The handle swipe `240 836 -> 300` always asks.
  - **Repeated form fields.** The scratch `pwam.py` builds a dict, so `wallpaper=1 wallpaper=2 ...` posts only the last
    value. Use a list (`postlist.py`).
  - **Android's large clock on a fresh image.** SystemUI shows its large (double-line) clock by default here. Step L's
    twin needs `settings put secure lockscreen_use_double_line_clock 0`.

## Findings

### RC3-1 (High): the boot cover's automatic ask is lost on Android 16, and the phone sits on SystemUI's clock with dead touches

**Fixed in** `rc3-fix` (design 59d §8.14): an ask with no answer is asked again at 3, 6 and 12 s, then every 12 s. Six boots showed the pad with no key press (`2026-10-23-rc3-fix-run.md`).

What the kid sees after every reboot (a1 `adb reboot`, c1 `svc power reboot`, b2 kill + `adb reboot`, and k1 with a
call): SystemUI's large clock and lock icon drawn over the cover's night mark (`14-...`). There is no pad and no
"Tap to unlock" line. A tap and a swipe up do nothing, and neither does a shade pull. This lasts until the screen times
out (~2 min) or the power key is pressed. After that every ask works (`15-...`).

The log:

```
13:22:08.659 BootCover: Boot cover asks for Android's PIN pad (relaunched, automatic 1 of 3)
13:22:08.689 ActivityTaskManager: Activity requesting to dismiss Keyguard: ActivityRecord{... BootCoverActivity t2}
13:22:10.473 PrimaryBouncerInteractor: PrimaryBouncerInteractor#show is being called before the primaryBouncerDelegate is set. Let's exit early ...
13:22:11.684 KeyguardTransitionRepository: FINISHED transition: ... from=OFF, to=LOCKSCREEN ... (boot)
```

The first ask, at the cover's first resume, and the ask after the theme overlay's relaunch both come 1-3 s before
SystemUI's keyguard finishes booting (OFF -> LOCKSCREEN). SystemUI drops them without calling back: no
`onDismissCancelled` and no `onDismissError`. So step F's 1 s error retry never runs, and the cover keeps the ask open
(a bare face, no line). SystemUI then sits in LOCKSCREEN while WindowManager says `mKeyguardOccluded=true`. Touches go
to SystemUI's `NotificationShade` (`input_interaction: ... NotificationShade`), not to the cover, and SystemUI's own
swipe-up doesn't open its bouncer in this state. The swipe tested was one that opens SystemUI's pad on its own face.

In k1 one tap did reach the cover, but in a1 and b2 none did. The Android 15 step M run didn't see this; this is the
first run of step F on Android 16.

**Proposed fix (BootCoverActivity):**
- A dead-ask re-ask: an ask with no answer after ~3 s is asked again, uncounted and part of the same ask, at 3, 6 and
  12 s while the gate holds. A second `requestDismissKeyguard` while the bouncer is already up is harmless.
- Don't wait for the focus: the glance's `askDead` needs our window focused, which is never true here.
- Optionally delay the first automatic ask until the cover has had its first frame plus ~2 s at boot.

Check it on the Jelly Star before tagging: its SystemUI may be ready sooner, but a lost ask is fatal for a kid who
doesn't know to press power.

### RC3-2 (Medium, look): the pad's success still slides - the glance's ground slides down before Home

**Fixed in** `rc3-fix` (§8.14): the glance shows the wallpaper while it asks, so the leave is the wallpaper-intra transition. The frames where the ground moves went from 3 to 0.

With a photo ground the hand-over is visible:
1. The pad fades (3-4 frames).
2. The ground (3-6 frames).
3. The whole ground slides down out of the screen in 3-4 frames (~170 ms), uncovering Home from the top. The ridge in
   the photo goes 522 -> 560 -> 650 -> 712 px.
4. Home (`07-...`, `08-...`).

Home's own start is unanimated (`flg=0x10010000`), and Home's ground lines up exactly with the glance's (the same
ridge, y 522). The moving surface is the closing glance in Home's OPEN transition, as the keyguard goes from occluded to
gone (`OCCLUDED -> GONE` finishes ~0.5 s later). Round 1's device run used the plain night ground, where a slide of a
flat colour can't be seen, so "no slide" was measured on a frame that couldn't show it. In p1 one frame of the full page
also showed under the pad's white fade, and Home's tiles filled in over 4 frames (three tiles, then the grid).

**Proposed fix:** give the closing side no animation as well:
- either `ActivityOptions.makeCustomAnimation(ctx, 0, 0)` on Home's start (it sets the exit animation of the task going
  behind),
- or `overrideActivityTransition(OVERRIDE_TRANSITION_OPEN, 0, 0)` on the glance as well as the CLOSE one, before the
  leave.

Re-record with a photo ground. A shell transition trace (`dumpsys activity service SystemUIService WMShell transitions`)
would show which handler animates the closing glance.

### RC3-3 (Medium): an ANR dialog when Back and Home are pressed on the cover before the take-over (slow unlocked setup)

**Fixed in** `rc3-fix` (§8.14), the narrow part: the cover starts no Home before the take-over and holds Back until it. The main thread's part stays open (RC3-4, PLAN's fix backlog).

c1 went like this:
1. The PIN, then Back 1 s later. Back finished the cover (`app-request`).
2. The system's HOME started a new cover (this boot was cover-first). The cover's own Home start disabled it on its way
   out (`HOME would resolve to the cover`).
3. That `PACKAGE_CHANGED` force-finished both cover records and made the policy engine drop the cover's PPA (one
   removal, re-added 4 s later by `Boot cover enabled (policy)`).
4. Home was started while the main process was still in `Unlocked setup took 8170 ms` on its main thread. The Home key
   at +2.5 s had no focused window to go to, so `am_anr: Input dispatching timed out (Application does not have a
   focused window)` for HomeActivity.
5. The system's "Application Not Responding: Vibb One" dialog took the focus for 2 minutes, until the next reboot.

**Proposed fix:**
- The cover doesn't start Home (its own Home start, or the HOME restart) before the main process has written the
  take-over stamp; until then it keeps itself in front.
- The unlocked setup's heavy part moves off the main thread, so HomeActivity can draw within the ANR window.

### RC3-4 (Low): the boot's PIN-to-Home gap is 12.6-16.4 s on this emulator

**Open**: PLAN's fix backlog (measure it on the release build on the Jelly Star).

`Unlocked setup took` 4556-9648 ms, and `Boot Home started` 12.6-16.4 s after the unlock reached the process. The kid
sees the cover's mark all that time. The main process logs dozens of `Verification of ... took 100-700 ms` lines (the
debug build's dex isn't verified ahead of time after an `adb install`), and the rc.2 runs had 2-3 s on the same AVD. So
this is likely debug and emulator cost, but it makes RC3-3 easy to hit. Measure it on the Jelly Star with the release
build.

### RC3-5 (Low): after the launcher is killed in the dark, the first wake shows SystemUI's face for ~1.6 s

**Open**: not in this round.

The restarted process started the glance while dark (Q59d-1 is fixed in that sense). But the activity was created only
at the wake (`onCreate` 1.4 s after the restart request, first draw 2.6 s after the screen came on). SystemUI's face
showed until then, followed by the glance's open animation with 5 black frames (`06-...`). Possible fix: start the
glance with no animation (`FLAG_ACTIVITY_NO_ANIMATION`, or `overrideActivityTransition` on its open) when it starts in
the dark.

### RC3-6 (Low/Note): `OWN_KEYPAD` costs the fingerprint

**Noted** in design 59d §8.4's fingerprint paragraph (`rc3-fix`).

When Android's credential is removed by hand, Android deletes the enrolled fingerprints. Our next Child PIN re-sets
Android's PIN, but the fingerprint stays gone until the parent enrols it again during a pause. Worth one line in the
docs and on the PWA's Lock page.

### RC3-7 (Low/Note): the pad shrinks with font and display size on this SystemUI

**Open**: for the Jelly Star's AOSP bouncer (the aosp-lock-look report).

The table above. "Display size one notch up" isn't a remedy for "den er bare snål liten" on the Pixel SystemUI: at 280
dpi the keys are clipped. The Jelly Star's AOSP bouncer is the one to judge.

### RC3-8 (Note): font 1.0, music and three rows - two rows out of sight with no hint

**Open**: a later item in PLAN's fix backlog (`rc3-fix`).

Fit candidate 3 shows the full tile and one row. The other two rows are below it in the scroll, with nothing of them
visible and no "more" line (the line only counts rows beyond three). This is by design 25 §1.2, but the kid can't tell
that two rows exist. At 1.3 the compact tile shows the next row under the fade.

### Observed, not findings

- **N2 is intermittent.** The overlays were off after boot a1 and on after b2.
- **N3, the emulator's stale first frame**, came in 5 of 8 wakes. It was the pre-provisioning Pixel wallpaper with a
  12:26 status bar.
- **Android 16 HOME behaviour.** On this image the Pixel launcher is direct-boot aware. Even so, the cover was the
  boot's HOME in every boot, and the stock launcher never showed.
- **A notice wake needs a new row.** An SMS to a conversation already shown on the glance (an update of the same
  notification) doesn't wake the screen. That is the `glanceWakes` rule, by design. A new conversation does wake it.

## Verdict

Don't tag `launcher-v0.36.0-rc.3` yet. **RC3-1 blocks it.** On Android 16 the boot cover's automatic ask was lost in
every boot of this run. The phone then shows SystemUI's clock over the cover with dead touches until the kid presses
power or the screen times out. That reads as a frozen phone after every restart, and it is new with step F on this
Android version.

A dead-ask re-ask in the cover (RC3-1's fix) is small and should go in first, followed by a short re-check here and on
the Jelly Star. Also before rc.3:
- RC3-3: the ANR dialog in the take-over window, reachable on a slow unlock. At least confirm it on the release build on
  the phone.
- RC3-2: the remaining slide, which the user will see in the success recording.

Everything else of rounds 1 and 2, J, L, §8.9, step M and the RC-to-RC fix behaved as designed on Android 16:
- No way past the PIN was found.
- The stock launcher or the chooser never showed.
- The fingerprint behaves as Android's own.
- K's ground matches SystemUI's frames.
- The tripped guard boots cleanly.
- The report claims `same_code_update_v1`.
