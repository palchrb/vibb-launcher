## Setup notes

- The debug feed's `library.json` was written by hand from `server/testdata/music_library.json`'s shape (two `rss`
  entries, `lan: true`, `cover: null`, `items` = the listing version); `items/6.json` and `items/7.json` from
  `fake-audio.py --print-listing`. The app loaded it at the receiver's nudge ("library: loaded, 2 entries").
- Reinstalling the APK with `install -r` after a stop/start of the AVD with its data kept lost the app's external files
  (the debug feed) and its hearts; the feed was pushed again and the hearts set again. Keep that in mind for §6s.
- Compose UI driven with `uiautomator dump` (content-desc) and `input tap`; a dump right after a screen change is
  sometimes empty - wait and dump again before concluding anything.
