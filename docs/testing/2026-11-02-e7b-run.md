## Findings

### Medium

- **M-C1 - the placed call's PROMOTE never comes during the call; the hour only at the hang-up.** Repro: own mode, kiosk on, a
  no-calls school rule; Lock › Emergency call › CALL 112 (the system dialer shows it; our in-call service isn't bound), let it
  run 3 min: no `PlacedCallWatch` line, the record stays provisional (10 min from the placing). Only the hang-up's idle gives
  `PROMOTE_AND_STOP`. Read in the code (`PlacedCallWatch.start`): the one evidence timer is posted at the placing +
  `PLACED_CONNECT_EVIDENCE_MS + 500` (20.5 s), but `PlacedCallEvidence` counts from the first off-hook, which came later
  (Telecom `CONNECTING -> DIALING` 21:25:29.586, the watch started 21:25:29.100, so off-hook ~0.5 s+ after it); at the timer
  `now - offhookSince < 20 s` -> NONE, and no report or timer follows in a steady call. On a real phone the off-hook delay
  is likely longer, so the timer always misses. Consequences: a call longer than 10 min whose launcher process dies (or the
  phone reboots) before the hang-up keeps only the provisional window, which has expired by then; the status report shows the
  provisional end during the call; C9 (restart after a PROMOTE) can't happen mid-call. Fix: post the check at the first
  off-hook + 20 s (or re-post it from `offhook()`), not from the placing.
- **M-B1 - a resume loop of the glance after the lock mode and the kiosk changed together while LOCKED under Home's lock task.**
  Repro (after the C7 reboot): own mode, kiosk on, LOCKED (Home's task the lock-task root); PWA lock mode -> Android and
  `kiosk_desired = 0` + a nudge (21:51:14), then sleep/wake: the glance resumes, logs `lock task: mode=1 features=0x31` and
  `the glance's own stopped`, pauses (`wm_failed_to_pause`), is destroyed (`finish-imm:clear-task-all`) and started again -
  **156, 266, 142, 250 "the glance resumed" lines a minute** 21:51-21:54 (63 and 47 in the next two, then it stopped by
  21:56:06); `LOCKED` throughout (Home's task); SystemUI's keyguard stays **un**occluded (`setOccluded(false)`, Android's own
  clock face, `b-loop-android-keyguard-face.png`), a swipe never brought the bouncer, and the screen kept going off within
  seconds; only `wm dismiss-keyguard` showed the PIN pad. Kiosk on again (21:56:46) healed it at once (`mode=1 features=0x71`,
  the glance occluding). Not clear how much of the rate my own wake/swipe attempts drove (I was poking it the whole time), nor
  whether the boot's ANR storm 4 min earlier mattered. On the phone the kiosk can't be switched from the PWA any more, so the
  path is the lock mode switched to Android while the kiosk is off for another reason (Home still rooting the lock task from
  before). Worth a look: `STOP_OWN` from the glance when the root is Home's task, and the refront restarting the glance
  outside the locked task.

### Low

- **L1 - Home from the ringing screen bounces back on the unlocked time-rule screen.** Repro (A7): unlocked on the time-rule
  screen, the time-rule screen has sent the ringing screen; Home -> HomeActivity -> a **new** LockActivity
  (`wm_finish_activity ... LockActivity,clear-task-stack`, `wm_create_activity` 21:23:34.768) whose fresh `alarmScreenSent` is
  false, so it sends again after 1.5 s (21:23:36.3); repeated until the per-screen-on cap (sends 21:24:08, :11, :15), then
  the row. The code QA's "the resume right after a send from it sends nothing" holds only for the same instance. Harmless
  (bounded by the cap, and the ringing screen is where the kid should be), but the flag could live in the runtime instead of
  the activity.

### Observations

- Without notification access an allowed clock's ring doesn't come over the own-mode lock at all (the full-screen intent is
  launched by our send, not by the platform, over the lock task); DeskClock's main screen, reached through the Clock row
  after the PIN, can stop it (the alarm's switch).
- Telecom's emergency callback mark (`ecm=true`) lasted at least 8 min after the last emergency call on this image (C4, C5),
  longer than the ~5 min the notes assume; our own window was therefore never tested alone within the hour. Device check.
- `adb reboot` on this image restarted system_server mid-boot (`FATAL EXCEPTION IN SYSTEM PROCESS: android.fg`,
  `DeadSystemException`) and gave the usual ANR storm.
