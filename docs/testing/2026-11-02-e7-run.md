## Findings

### High

- **H1 - the emergency callback is rejected under a no-calls rule** (main's call code; found during Stille). Repro: own
  mode, kiosk on, a school rule with calls off; the lock's Emergency call -> "Call 112?" -> CALL (the emulator's 112
  connects, here 48 s) -> End call; `adb emu gsm call +4799887766` 13 s and 70 s later. Telecom skips screening (ECBM:
  `onCallFilteringComplete: allow call`), then `KidInCallService: Rejecting an incoming call` -> `REJECTED`. The callback
  window never opened: no `call_boot_state` record (Telecom bound the system dialer through
  `EmergencyInCallServiceConnection` for the 112 call and our process logged nothing for it), and Telecom wrote no call-log
  row for the 112 call (`LogCall; logged` for every other call, none for it - AOSP keeps emergency numbers out of the call
  log unless the carrier config allows them), so the call-log path has nothing to read. **Device check on the Jelly
  Star first** (is 112 logged there, does our in-call service see the call); if it holds, a rescue service calling the
  child back during school is turned away.

### Medium

- **M1 - a ring that is still going can become unstoppable under a rule.** (a) After the 5th re-send the lock stays in
  front while the alarm keeps ringing; nothing on the glance stops it. (b) After a PIN unlock under the rule the time-rule
  screen doesn't bring the ringing screen back (the re-send lives only in the PIN lock), the shade is shut with the kiosk
  on and Recents is empty. Repro (own mode, kiosk on, a non-silent school rule): an alarm rings over the lock, press power
  six times (each a sleep and a wake) -> from the 6th the lock stays; or type the PIN while it rings -> the time-rule
  screen, the alarm ringing (`Firing`, USAGE_ALARM player) with nothing to tap (`a2-unlocked-timerule-alarm-capped.png`,
  `a3-recents-from-timerule-ringing.png`). Here it rang until DeskClock's own 10-minute silence (17:15-17:25); the kid's
  only way out of (b) was to lock and wake again while sends were left. The bug's own complaint ("alarmen ringer ... men man
  får ikke slått den av") is back in these two corners.
- **M2 - L2 doesn't fire: the kiosk turned on while LOCKED takes no lock task until the next resume.** Repro: Android mode,
  kiosk off, locked with the glance resumed (`NONE`); `kiosk_desired = 1` + a nudge: the policy is applied (kiosk packages
  in `LockTaskPolicy`) but `mLockTaskModeState=NONE` for 80 s+, `LOCKED` only after a screen cycle. Read in the code: the
  glance's `chromeListener` (the `chromeEntryRecheckDue` check) runs only from `PinLockRuntime.refreshChrome`, and a policy's
  `AppEnforcer.apply` -> `LockTaskChrome.applyPlan` never calls the chrome listeners. At the same moment `HomeActivity`
  logged `Lock task change failed - retried at the next resume / IllegalArgumentException: Invalid task, not in foreground`
  from `reconcileKioskMode` (Home tried `startLockTask` from the background).

### Low

- **L1 - a silenced alarm still lights the screen**: under a fully silent rule DeskClock wakes the device itself
  (`WAKE_REASON_APPLICATION ... deskclock:AlarmModel`); the lock shows, no sound, no ringing screen. The clock's behaviour,
  not ours; worth a look on the Jelly Star's clock (a lit screen in class every alarm).
- **L2 - the alarm's yield is logged as SOS first**: every ring logs `The lock left the front just after a screen-on:
  probably Emergency SOS` right before `Lock yields to an exempt screen: alarm`.
- **L3 - the lock-task-feature heal still runs without lock task**: kiosk off, Android mode: `Status bar heal (lock
  resumed)` / `(first screen-on)`: `lock-task features 49 -> 48 written` - a flip SystemUI never hears with no lock task
  (the R2 note says the screen-off heal replaces it there). Harmless, dead work.
- **L4 - §6z's setup doesn't work as written**: (a) the rights file gives no read on `/`, so the server's discovery
  PROPFIND gets 403 and the PWA says "The password was refused" (a `[root] user: .+ / collection: / permissions: R` section
  fixes it; the 403-as-password wording may mislead a parent too); (b) `MKCALENDAR .../skrivebeskyttet/` fails under the
  same rights (create it before the `ro` section is in force; Radicale reads the rights only at start); (c) the server URL
  must be the host's LAN address, not `10.0.2.2` (E6's L4 again); (d) "Pick skrivebeskyttet" can't be done - a read-only
  calendar isn't in the select; the orange line needs a calendar that turns read-only after the pick.
- **L5 - "Kalenderen tar ikke imot avtaler" outlived the fix**: with write access back the line and "1 venter på å bli
  skrevet" stayed for the minute watched (then the calendar was unticked). Maybe the backoff by design; say so on the page
  or retry at the next fetch.
- **L6 - a transient double status**: right after the first write "Skrevet 18:44 · 2 avtaler" and "2 venter på å bli
  skrevet" together; 30 s later only the first.
- **L7 - the time-rule card says "Only emergency calls work now." above a Calendar or Signal row**: it means calls, reads
  as "nothing else works".

## Emulator facts learned

- `svc power stayon true` doesn't keep the screen on against the policy's 60 s timeout; tap every 15 s or raise Lock ›
  Screen timeout for the session.

- With the screen on and unlocked, a full-screen alarm shows as a heads-up (Snooze/Stop), not its activity.
