## 1. The lock's media tile

**Fossify's PlaybackState** (`dumpsys media_session`): while PLAYING `actions=7340027` = STOP, PAUSE, REWIND,
**SKIP_TO_PREVIOUS, SKIP_TO_NEXT**, FAST_FORWARD, SET_RATING, SEEK_TO, PLAY_PAUSE; while PAUSED `7340029` (PLAY instead of
PAUSE); before the first play (NONE) `7339701` (STOP, PLAY, PREV, NEXT, RATING, PLAY_PAUSE). So both skips are
advertised and the tile sends them as media buttons.

Fossify playing "Spor 1" under the lock (shot 10). Every tap is logged by `MediaSessionService` as
`callingPackage:me.vibb.launcher.debug targetPackage:org.fossify.musicplayer reason:action=ACTION_DOWN;code=KEYCODE_MEDIA_*`
and its ACTION_UP - no `MediaSessionRecord:*` transport call for Fossify anywhere in the run:

- **Pause**: PAUSED at once (state change 2 ms after the key; shot 11). **Play**: PLAYING at once (shot 12).
- **Next**: "Spor 1" -> "Spor 2", exactly one skip, one DOWN/UP pair (shot 13).
- **Previous**: 15 s into "Spor 2" it restarts "Spor 2" (Media3's rule: past 3 s a previous restarts the track); two
  previous taps 0.5 s apart went "Spor 2" -> restart -> "Spor 1" (shot 15), one key pair per tap.
- **Pause, play, pause** within 0.55 s (three key pairs at .959, .229, .509): ends **PAUSED** and stays so over the next
  2.8 s; no fallback call followed (shot 16).
- **Play, pause, play** within 0.53 s: ends **PLAYING** (state changes PLAYING, PAUSED, PLAYING, then nothing; shot 17).
- QA M1's exact case, pause then play 0.5 s later: PAUSED at .994, PLAYING at 11.515, still PLAYING 2.5 s later - the
  first tap's 1 s check no longer undoes the second.

**Vibb music** ("E9-toner"): on the tile when playing (shot 21); pause, play, next ("Testlåt en" -> "Testlåt to"; shot
22) and previous all work, sent as transport controls (`MediaSessionRecord:pause/play/next/previous`), as designed for
Vibb. **Home's card** ("Spilles nå", E9-toner): its pause and play button work (shots 23-25).

**Fennec** playing an `<audio>` from `http://10.0.2.2:8088`: tile "E9 lyd · http://10.0.2.2:8088/page.html?..." with

## Findings

**L1 - The refusal text's example address breaks at a dot** (Low, cosmetic; `values-nb/strings.xml:205`, en
`values/strings.xml:257` `server_url_refused`). In the dialog at 320 dp, font 1.0, the line ends "som http://100" and
the next starts ".101.102.103)." (shots 42/43) - it reads as two things. A shorter example ("en Tailscale-IP (100.x.y.z)")
or word joiners (U+2060) after the dots would keep it on one line. The text is otherwise whole and right.

**Info.**
- **The tile shows previous/next for a session that advertises no skip** (Fennec): a tap sends one transport
  `next`/`previous` that the browser ignores, so the button looks dead. Pre-existing (the tile always draws all three);
  hiding or dimming the skips when `PlaybackState.actions` lacks them would be the fix if wanted
  (`MusicLockPanel.kt:64-65` binds both always; `view_lock_glance.xml:326`).
- **The 1 s fallback on a slow player** (Fennec under translation, above): one transport pause after the key went
  unanswered for a second; harmless (idempotent) and only for the newest tap (`MusicSessions.kt:376-379`). The fallback
  path for a fast player that ignores keys was not exercised (no such player on the image).
- With Fennec loaded the guest ran out of memory and Vibb music's and Fossify's processes were reclaimed (their sessions
  dropped to NONE); emulator only.
- SystemUI showed an ANR dialog right after boot (swiftshader, slow boot); dismissed with Wait. Environment.
- Procedure: `am force-stop` does not stop the device-owner launcher; `run-as me.vibb.launcher.debug kill -9 <pid>` does,
  for planting prefs.
