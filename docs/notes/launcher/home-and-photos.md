## The kid's own contact pictures (design 79 §5; branch `phone-book-list`)

- **What shows** (pure `calls/KidPhotos.kt` `shownPhoto`, `KidPhotosTest`): an emergency number none; else the kid's own
  picture (only while the contact's number is the one it was set for); else the parent's; else the initial. A kid's
  picture that can't be decoded gives way to the parent's (`ShownPhoto.Kid.fallback`, code QA L8). Every avatar
  binding goes through it - `KidAvatars.bindContact` (Home, the phone book, the card, the glance rows, the call screen,
  Meldinger) and the SMS notification's person; Home's call card and the call screen key their rebind on it.
- **Where** (`KidContactPhotos`): `filesDir/kid_contact_photos/<random>.jpg` (CE) and CE prefs `kid_contact_photos`
  (contact id -> file, number, set at). Never in DE storage, a report, an upload or a backup. One per contact, at most as
  many as there are contacts **on the phone** (what the prune keeps, code QA L7) and never more than `KID_PHOTOS_MAX`
  (100), each at most 150 KB; the cap is checked before the picker opens, with its own line ("Du har bilder på så
  mange kontakter som det er plass til. Fjern et bilde først.").
- **No disk in a binding** (QA H2): `KidContactPhotos.snapshot` is loaded on the store's own IO thread after the first
  unlock (empty before it); a binding reads the snapshot and `ContactPhotos`' memory only. The kid's JPEG decodes on
  `ContactPhotos`' thread under `kid:<file>` (`cachedKid`); a change of the published snapshot - only that, code QA
  L6 - calls `ContactPhotos.changed()`, so avatars rebind in place. The snapshot is loaded at the unlock's CE refresh
  (`Application` -> `CallPolicyStore.refresh` -> `afterRefresh`), before Home's first binding (code QA N4). A source check in `KidPhotosTest` pins it.
- **Pruned only on a CE refresh of managed rules** (`kidPhotosAfterRefresh`, from `CallPolicyStore.refresh` after the
  Element rooms): a record whose contact left the phone (`rules.contacts`) or whose number changed goes with its file;
  calls off, unknown or unmanaged rules keep them; files no record names are swept at each change.
- **Setting one:** a long-press on the card's photo (haptic, dimmed while held; TalkBack "Endre bilde av Mamma") opens
  "Bilde av …" (`KidPhotoSheet`: Velg bilde, Ta bilde only with a camera the parent allows, Fjern bildet ditt, "Bildet
  blir bare på denne telefonen."), offered only for a phone-book contact, not an emergency number, after the unlock,
  with a way to pick (`kidPhotoOffered`). The sheet closes with the card (code QA M1), so Home's `closeOverlays()` at
  the lock leaves nothing of it. `KidPhotoActivity` opens the picker or the camera at once
  (`ui/pictures/PicturePick.kt`, moved from `ui/messages/` and split: `decode` in the isolated process, the SMS
  `prepare` on top), frames the picture (`PhotoCropView`, pure `PhotoCrop`: cover, drag, pinch to
  `max(1, min(3, shortSide / 384))`, clamped) and re-encodes the square (`PictureEncoder.forContact`: 512 px, quality
  stepped to 150 KB, no EXIF). It checks the time rule before it opens anything (code QA L4), at resume, and watches it, finishes when a sync takes the
  contact out of the phone book or makes its number an emergency number, and is no free screen for screen time.
- **The contact request's picture** (§5.5): the form's draft in `cacheDir/request_photo_draft/` (swept after a day),
  renamed to `filesDir/contact_request_photos/<request id>.jpg` at Send (strays swept at the phone book's first resume, `draftsToSweep`: never the form's, never one younger than an hour); shown on the "Venter på svar" row; handed to
  the contact that arrives in the phone book unless the kid set one already (`reconcileContactRequests`' hand-overs,
  `KidContactPhotos.adopt`), deleted on every other outcome (only an open request keeps one, `requestPhotosToKeep`).
- **List-sized photos** (§3.7, QA N8): the rows, Home's contacts and the glance rows take a copy at most 160 px
  (`ContactPhotos.cached(.., thumb = true)`, key `<hash>@160`); the card and the call screen the full square.

## Home at boot, Home roots lock task, no "App is not available" (design 16, `docs/design/16-boot-home.md`, at the monorepo root)

- **Typed HOME only**: every "bring Home" start goes through `lock/HomeFront` (`MAIN` + `HOME`, `setPackage(ours)`, no component) - an explicit `Intent(ctx, HomeActivity)` makes a STANDARD Home task next to the HOME-typed one (`HomeFrontTest` bans it).
- **Boot** (`lock/BootHome`, pure `bringHomeAtBoot`): before the CE unlock only direct-boot-aware HOMEs resolve, so a stock launcher (Pixel's) was Home for 3-5 s. The first process start of a boot (BOOT_COUNT vs CE prefs `boot_home`, stored after Home started) brings Home from `PinLockRuntime.init`'s ProcessStart when apps are managed, the kiosk is on or the PIN lock is active, never over a call; the lock is then the 1 s fallback (`LockStep.showLockLater`). `HomeActivity` stays non-direct-boot-aware.
- **Kiosk on: Home roots lock task, never the lock** (pure `lockTaskEntry`/`lockLeave`, `lock/LockTaskRoot.kt`): a lock resumed without lock task starts Home (whose resume calls `startLockTask` and shows the lock), at most every 3 s, and starts lock task itself only as the 1 s fallback. The lock always leaves through Home - Home is started **before** `finishAndRemoveTask`, so the stock launcher's latent Recents task (a BlockedAppActivity in kiosk) never surfaces; a refused finish means the lock is the root (`rootLeave`: stop, Home, finish). Kiosk off: the lock stops its own lock task (as Android's face with Android locked it takes none, design 59b R2 - `lock-and-glance.md`) and, since design 32, leaves through Home too unless a call is live (`lockLeave(..., callLive)`). `HomeActivity.reconcileKioskMode` catches `startLockTask` failures.
- **Kiosk features and the recents provider (16d decision 2)**: design 16's `kioskFeatures` (OVERVIEW dropped with the app-block bit) is **reverted** - the user's rule: every stock gesture keeps working in the kiosk, never remove one without asking concretely. The plan sets the server's features. Instead the system's recents provider - the package of `config_recentsComponentName` read through `Resources.getSystem()` (`AppEnforcer.recentsPackage`, null-safe), never a fixed name - is a kiosk-block helper (`HelperKind.RECENTS`): pure `lockTaskHelpers` keeps it only when it is a system app and not Settings/the camera/Play, the plan pins it whenever the kiosk is on with the block bit, also during a time-rule lock. Trusted only as a system app with a HOME activity or the configured recents activity, never SystemUI/`android`/Play (pure `trustedRecentsProvider`, `AppEnforcer.systemRecentsPackage`; qa-16d-code #5). **Out of the kiosk list while the PIN lock is LOCKED** (qa-16d-code #1: a keyboard's Recents key, an accessibility action or a desynced SystemUI showed the Overview's snapshots over the lock): the plan's `recentsPin` goes to `LockTaskChrome.applyPlan`, `lockTaskWhileLocked(..., recentsPin)` drops it on the LOCKED edge (clearing quickstep's RecentsActivity task) and the unlocked pass pins it again; before the first apply `prefetchRecentsPin` and `fallbackKioskPackages` do the same. LOCKED still drops HOME/OVERVIEW/NOTIFICATIONS (113). Residual: a Recents start while LOCKED leaves a stale BlockedAppActivity task; the lock leaving through Home keeps it from surfacing at the unlock, the first swipe after it may show it once. Every caller passes the provider (`RecentsProviderCallersTest`). Quickstep's RecentsActivity then starts inside lock task: no "App is not available" from the swipes, the hold or the Recents key (experiment 2 variant A: 0 dialogs in 6 pinned boots and a force-stop). Not pinned by the kiosk-off PIN lock (no block bit there except during a VoIP call, so no dialog; pinned it would open the stock home to the call app over the lock). Each apply with the bit logs `Recents provider (config_recentsComponentName): <pkg>, system <bool>, pinned|not pinned`. **Never restricted by enforcement**: `AppEnforcer.systemRecentsPackage` (system only) is in the plan's never-restrict set (allowlist, SMS off, time-rule lock), and `shouldSuspendNewPackage` and `cameraLockTargets` leave it alone, so a stock launcher with a launcher icon never loses quickstep to `apply()`; Home and the drawer still leave it out (`AppFilter.kidListable`, like Play outside install mode). **The update fence is the only thing that suspends it** (16d guard 2; `fencePlan(..., recentsPackage)` fences it although pinned/protected or listed as controllable, the release and the orphan sweep unsuspend it): on Pixel it is the stock launcher, the fallback Home the fence stops, and the night-time update can do without Recents for a minute. Accepted residual risk (16d decisions, unless the user vetoes): an allowed app could start that package's stock home, settings or search explicitly; HOME stays ours (persistent preferred), and Overview's actions (Pause app, Markup via Screenshot/Edit) end in BlockedAppActivity. `smoke-test.sh` checks no BlockedAppActivity after the unlock, on Recents and on a swipe-up. Not device-tested: the 16 and 16d docs' "Implementation status".
- **Boot cover (16b, server switch `boot_cover`, off by default)**: `lock/BootCoverActivity` - direct-boot-aware (in `DirectBootComponents`), own process `:bootcover` where `Application.onCreate` returns at once, a plain framework `Activity` with the breathing Vibb mark, no CE/native/lock task/services; HOME filter at priority 0 plus its own PPA (extra category `BOOT_COVER`, next to HomeActivity's, never clearing it). `android:enabled="false"`: in own mode the main process enables it only at `ACTION_SHUTDOWN` (runtime receiver) while wanted (pure `bootCoverEnabled`), and disables it at its first start after the unlock (before Home's typed start) and whenever the switch is off (android mode: step M below); the cover disables itself once unlocked and shown 3 s (16e) and then finishes (FallbackHome-style), and at its 2nd crash in a boot. Crash guard `lock/BootCoverGuard` (installed first in the `:bootcover` branch of `Application.onCreate`; one DE AtomicFile, `CoverRecord`): a trip is sticky - no arming at shutdown until the switch goes off (deletes the record) and on. The arm is written `SYNCHRONOUS` and `HomeFront` starts no Home after `ACTION_SHUTDOWN`. Status `boot_cover` + capability `boot_cover_v1`. A crash reboot = A+B only; `adb reboot` sends no `ACTION_SHUTDOWN` (use the power menu or `svc power reboot`). **Android mode (design 59 F3, "F3" in the 59 design doc)**: with Android's PIN the boot stays BFU until the kid types it, and the PIN's dismissal revealed the stock launcher until our `initRest` + `BootHome` (12.6 s on the debug build). A tripped guard gets one more try after a launcher update (`CoverRecord.trippedVersion`, and since qa-rc-to-rc-code R2R-6 `trippedVersionName`: an RC over an RC of the same code counts - old-rule builds only) or the switch's off -> on edge (`coverGuardClearDue`, F3C-3; the PWA's note still needs the server to gate on the reported `wanted`). Then `BootCallReceiver` (`LOCKED_BOOT_COMPLETED`, after the call policy) calls `BootCover.atLockedBoot` (main thread, DE only; pure `coverAtLockedBoot`), decided right there (f3-run B1: before the PIN our process is cached and frozen, and a **runtime** receiver's broadcast stays deferred until the PIN - never wait on one before the unlock): nothing without the marker, unlocked, a tripped guard or after our take-over (`BootCover.tookOver`, F3C-6); a live call (`BootHome.callLive`) is retried every 5 s by an alarm (exact while `canScheduleExactAlarms()`; no cap - the unlock bounds it; the cancel uses `FLAG_NO_CREATE`) to `BootCallReceiver`'s explicit `ACTION_COVER_RETRY` (a manifest receiver reaches a frozen process; F3C-1); else it enables the cover and starts the typed HOME - **after every boot, the shutdown's arm included** (qa-f3-round2-code F3R-2: the start brings the cover over whatever the boot started, the system's chooser too; what HOME resolves to is only logged, `Locked boot: HOME resolves to ...`), which before the unlock resolves to the cover alone - so `adb reboot`, crash reboots and a missed shutdown arm get it too (a device owner may start from the background). The screen isn't read for the decision (logged with the start: `Boot cover started at the locked boot (android mode, interactive ...)`, F3R-4). Residual: a keyguard emergency dialpad open when the start comes - usually 1-5 s after the boot on the emulator, no bound (one came 15 s after our Home) - goes behind the keyguard (the kid opens it again; ## App names and icons (design 14, `docs/design/14-app-display.md`, at the monorepo root)
